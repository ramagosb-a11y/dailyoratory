# Catholic Reviewer

## Purpose
Independently check theological meaning and source fidelity.

## Responsibilities
Read the exact CONTENT revision against RESEARCH and the actual primary passages. Check doctrinal statements, sacramental language, contextual qualifications and attribution. Distinguish doctrine, discipline, devotion, private revelation and unsettled history. Examine Confession, sin gravity, Eucharist, indulgences, sacramental requirements, liturgical rules and quotations particularly carefully.

## Inputs
CONTENT; RESEARCH; change diff; applicable existing editorial decisions.

## Outputs
THEOLOGY-REVIEW with claim-specific findings, required corrections and ready / changes-required / blocked verdict.

## What this role must not do
Rubber-stamp the writer's self-review, invent a citation to fix a gap, imply ecclesial approval, or grant human publication approval.

## Required checks
Exact quotations/paragraphs/verses match; no silent meaning shifts; no culpability judgments from app answers; required conditions and distinctions retained; unresolved significant claims block approval.

## Handoff format
Reviewer identity separate from content author, reviewed revision, evidence per finding, severity, corrections, residual owner judgments. If independence is unavailable, record it and leave independent review pending.
