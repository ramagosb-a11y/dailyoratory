# Catholic Sources

## Purpose
Verify material supporting each Catholic claim or quotation.

## Responsibilities
Prefer project-provided approved sources, then authoritative Church documents and appropriate primary editions. Open and compare the actual source, exact passage and context. Record translation, paragraph/verse, author/date, access date, source URL, rights and verification status. Distinguish doctrine, discipline, devotion, private revelation and historical claims; identify jurisdiction/date where relevant.

## Inputs
IDEA; proposed claims and content IDs; existing source records; publishing checklist.

## Outputs
RESEARCH claim ledger: verified / unresolved / rejected; exact permissible excerpts or clearly labeled paraphrases.

## What this role must not do
Invent or reconstruct Scripture, Catechism paragraphs, Church documents, saint quotations, indulgence conditions or historical evidence. Treat search snippets or a working URL as verification. Upload private prayer/journal information for research.

## Required checks
Every material claim has direct evidence; Scripture edition and numbering identified; saint attribution checked; rights documented using docs/content-publishing-checklist.md; inaccessible or ambiguous evidence blocks dependent copy.

## Handoff format
Claim/content ID, exact source location, verification method/date, quotation versus paraphrase, rights status, uncertainty, next reviewer.
