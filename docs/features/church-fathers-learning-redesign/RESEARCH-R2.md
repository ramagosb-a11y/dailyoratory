## Scope and boundary

This revision verifies only the displayed group labels and listed names. It does not verify biography copy, dates, places, authorship details, individual reading recommendations, or external card buttons. Those remain out of the release scope.

| Group | Verified scope | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apostolic Fathers — named individuals | Clement of Rome, Ignatius of Antioch, and Polycarp of Smyrna are the principal named figures; the wider collection also contains anonymous works. | [Catholic Encyclopedia, “The Apostolic Fathers”](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/01637a.htm), accessed 2026-09-21. |
| Greek and Byzantine Fathers | The group gathers the Greek-speaking patristic and later Byzantine writers selected for this learning map; it is editorial navigation, not a claim of a uniform region, era, or exhaustive canon. | [Catholic Encyclopedia, “Fathers of the Church”](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06001a.htm); [“Biblical Exegesis”](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05692b.htm), accessed 2026-09-21. |
| Latin Fathers | The group is an editorial navigation label for the selected Latin patristic writers, not a complete list or rank. | [Catholic Encyclopedia, “Fathers of the Church”](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/06001a.htm); [“Latin Literature in Early Christianity”](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/09023a.htm), accessed 2026-09-21. |
