## Method and rights boundary

The new page uses original orientation and guide copy. It contains no quotation, reproduced translation, or excerpt from a patristic source. External reading buttons link to source pages rather than copying their text. Daily Oratory verifies only that each outbound page was accessible on the date listed; a link is not a permission to reproduce the translation or page framing. No text from those pages is reproduced here.

## Exclusions and safeguards

- Do not use *Martyrdom of Polycarp* as Polycarp's authored work.
- Do not include Second Clement as securely Clement of Rome's work.
- Do not include Tertullian in the launch pathway; review him separately if later added.
- Do not make a topic door for Confession, Baptism, Mary, apostolic succession, or authority until a narrower content/source review has selected contextually adequate evidence.
- The launch pathway does not claim an exhaustive list of Fathers, periods, languages, regions, or “Great Fathers.” The existing archive is not yet source-certified for broad reuse.
