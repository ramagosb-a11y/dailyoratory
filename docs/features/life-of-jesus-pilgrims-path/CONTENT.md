## Formation flow

1. Hero invitation: “Walk the Gospel road with Jesus—from the promises of salvation and the Incarnation to the Cross, Resurrection, and Ascension.”
2. Accuracy note: “The Gospels proclaim one Lord but do not always arrange every teaching, journey, and appearance in the same order. This path distinguishes explicit sequence, traditional harmony, and unresolved order.”
3. Chronology legend and Catholic-category legend, visible before the first record and available throughout the page.
4. Fifteen-era pilgrimage path, with 18 chapter landmarks and 280 classified source records.
5. Major-era image transitions used as contemplative pauses, never as evidence for an uncertain date, location, or witnessed viewpoint.
6. Each record shows title, concise original summary, exact reference as visible text, Douay–Rheims reading action, chronology label, Catholic category where applicable, parallel-source panels, and a restrained uncertainty note.
7. Conclusion at the Ascension: the apostles worship and return to Jerusalem; no invented prayer or unsourced quotation is added in v1.
